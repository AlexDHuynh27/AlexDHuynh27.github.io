About me

The first time the urge to create called to me was when I was 10 years old. Adults had always asked me, as they do with all kids, what I wanted to do when I grew up and at the time I figured I like video games and video games are made by coding, so I had always said Software Engineer. But, it wasn't until my parents had signed me up for a robotics class that I could confidently say I wanted to be a softare engineer. I remember being sad as I realized I was the only one in the class. The instructor came in and handed me an instruction book and a bunch of lego like parts. I put the robot together and then called the instructor back. He then said I could code it and so I did off of the insturctions, and it was the greatest thing to see the robot I had made move around with a controller. It was from that moment that I knew that I knew what I wanted to do.
